# Design System: Paris Fête Foraine (1900 Universal Exposition)

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## World Identity
- **Concept**: Paris 1900 Universal Exposition & Vintage French Carousel / Fête Foraine.
- **Tone**: Cultured, elegant, sunlit, theatrical yet scientifically rigorous. Warm paper, antique brass, terracotta carmine, and orangerie sage. Absolutely zero neon colors.
- **Signature Move**: **Carousel Counterweight & High-Wire Equilibrium Scale**: Health Factor visualized as an acrobatic balance beam shifting between the safety net (equilibrium) and the liquidation drop.

## Palette
- **Canvas Ground**: `#fcfbf7` (Warm Linen Cream)
- **Surface Elevation**: `#ffffff` (Chalk Paper with soft warm shadow)
- **Primary / Brand**: `#c2410c` (Terracotta Carmine)
- **Antique Accent**: `#b45309` / `#d97706` (Antique Brass / Warm Ochre)
- **Safety / Equilibrium**: `#15803d` / `#166534` (Orangerie Sage & Olive Green)
- **Caution / Tension**: `#d97706` / `#fef3c7` (Amber Ochre)
- **Critical / Liquidation**: `#be123c` / `#9f1239` (Vintage Carmine Crimson)
- **De-leveraging / Action**: `#1d4ed8` / `#1e40af` (French Deck Blue)
- **Typography Ink**: `#0f172a` (Deep Slate / Printers Charcoal), `#334155` (Muted Ink)
- **Borders & Rules**: `rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.18)` (Subtle Brass Filament)

## Typography
- **Display & Headings**: `Playfair Display`, `Cinzel`, serif with high editorial authority.
- **UI Copy & Controls**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, clean legible humanist sans.
- **Numbers & Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono`, crisp tabular figures for collateral amounts, Health Factors, and price shocks.

## Component Language
- **Cards**: `paper-card` with delicate 1px brass borders (`border-amber-900/15`) and soft ambient elevation (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`).
- **Badges**: Stamped heritage pill labels with subtle dashed borders or warm tinted backgrounds.
- **Inputs & Sliders**: Warm terracotta accent rails (`accent-[#c2410c]`), custom ivory track backgrounds.
- **Buttons**: Rich terracotta carmine (`bg-[#c2410c] text-white hover:bg-[#9a3412]`) and antique brass borders.
